About this project

Why SIM exists, and how it fits together.

Campus communities — students in hostels, staff on late shifts, and landlords housing students off-campus — are frequently affected by security incidents that go unreported because people don't know who to tell, fear retaliation, or simply have no fast way to raise an alert. SIM (Security Incident Mapping) is a mini web platform built to close that gap: a simple place to report what happened, optionally stay anonymous, and see what's being reported nearby so the whole community stays aware.

This is a prototype built for the SEN202 Weekend 1 assignment. It demonstrates the core reporting and feed workflow using the browser's local storage in place of a real backend database. The accompanying project documentation — the Ishikawa root-cause analysis, the Use Case diagram, the Software Requirements Specification, and the community questionnaire — are all included in the project's GitHub repository.

Who this is for

Students

On & off campus

Report incidents in hostels or off-campus lodges, and check the feed before walking home at night.

Staff

Day & night shifts

Flag incidents encountered while on duty, especially during late hours around campus.

Landlords

Off-campus properties

Report incidents around your property and stay looped in on nearby activity affecting your tenants.
