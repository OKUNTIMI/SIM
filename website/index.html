How it works

Three steps, no complexity — built for people in a hurry.

Step 1

Report

Pick a category, describe what happened, and drop a pin on the location — anonymously if you prefer.

Step 2

Verify

Campus Security reviews the report and marks it Verified, or escalates serious cases to law enforcement.

Step 3

Stay aware

Everyone sees a live, filterable feed of verified incidents — no more relying on rumours.
